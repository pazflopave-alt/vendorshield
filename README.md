- **Live Application:** [vendorshield.vercel.app](https://vercel.com) *(In Active Development)*
- **Interactive Sandbox:** 1-click demo mode with pre-loaded vendor compliance audits.

---

## 🎯 Core Enterprise Capabilities

- 📄 **Automated Document Auditing:** Parses vendor contracts, DPAs, and compliance certificates (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR) to flag high-risk clauses with exact source citations.
- 📊 **Audited Risk Scoring Engine:** Calculates a multi-factor Vendor Risk Score across Legal, Security, and Financial health dimensions.
- 🔐 **Strict RBAC & Multi-Tenant Workspaces:** Role-Based Access Control (`Admin`, `Compliance Officer`, `Auditor`, `Vendor`) with organization isolation.
- 📜 **Immutable Audit Logs:** Tracks every document upload, AI evaluation, and manual approval for regulatory traceability.
- 💳 **B2B Subscription Billing:** Integrated Stripe Checkout and Webhooks for tiered enterprise plans.
